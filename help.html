Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place a bid? ▼
Go to the Auctions page, hover over an item, enter your bid amount (must be higher than current price), and click "Add Bid". Your bid will appear in "My Bids".
Can I cancel my bid? ▼
Yes! You can remove bids from the "My Bids" page by clicking the "Remove Bid" button. However, in real auctions, bids are usually final.
What happens if I win? ▼
If you have the highest bid when the auction ends, you'll win the item! We'll contact you via email to arrange payment and shipping.
How do I track my bids? ▼
Click on "My Bids" in the navigation menu to see all your active bids, their status (Pending, Winning, or Outbid), and total bid amount.
Is my payment information secure? ▼
Absolutely! We use industry-standard encryption and never store your payment details. All transactions are processed securely.
